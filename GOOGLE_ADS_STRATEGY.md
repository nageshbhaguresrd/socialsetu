## KEYWORD STRATEGY

### Negative Keywords (Shared List)
- Jobs, careers, salary, internship, course, training, free download, what is, login, template.

### Ad Group 1: Brand + Agency
- **Search Intent:** Transactional / Commercial
- **Exact:** [social media agency india], [smm agency delhi], [digital marketing agency mumbai]
- **Phrase:** "best social media agency india", "top smm company india"

### Ad Group 2: Problem-Aware
- **Search Intent:** Informational / Commercial
- **Exact:** [how to get more leads on instagram], [facebook ads not working india]
- **Phrase:** "business growth on social media india", "how to scale d2c brand india"

## MONTH 1 OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST
1. **Day 1-7:** Monitor Bid Strategy ("Learning" phase). Negative keyword additions daily.
2. **Day 14:** Review search terms. Exclude irrelevant city names if not targeting.
3. **Day 21:** Pause RSA headlines with poor (Low/Average) performance.
4. **Day 30:** Analyze CPL by industry/ad group. Reallocate budget to the "Winner" ad group.
