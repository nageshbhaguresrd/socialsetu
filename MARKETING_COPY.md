## BUDGET & STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. Recommended Daily Budget (INR)
- **Real Estate:** ₹3,000 - ₹5,000 (CPC is high, but LTV is massive).
- **D2C Brands:** ₹2,500 - ₹4,000 (Focus on creative testing).
- **Restaurants:** ₹1,000 - ₹2,000 (Keep it local, smaller radius).
- **IT/SaaS:** ₹2,000 - ₹3,500 (LinkedIn might be secondary, use Meta for authority).
- **Tier-2 SMBs:** ₹1,000 (Testing the waters).

### 2. Bid Strategy
- **Initial Phase (7 Days):** "Highest Volume" to give Meta's algorithm enough data to learn your converter profile.
- **Scaling Phase:** Switch to "Cost Cap" at 1.2x your target CPL to prevent budget leakage during weekend auctions.

### 3. A/B Testing Priority
1. **Hook (First 3s of video / First line of copy)**: This is 70% of the battle.
2. **Offer**: If "Free Audit" is slow, test "Free 15-min Mini Consultation" or "Free Growth Checklist".
3. **CTA Button**: Test "Learn More" vs "Sign Up" (Sign Up often works better in India for lead forms).

### 4. Expansion Plan
- Start with **D2C Brands** and **Real Estate** as they are the "hungriest" for results right now.
- Create a **1% Lookalike (LAL)** audience once you hit 100 conversions (pixel fires).
- Target **"Broad"** (No interests, just age/location) for D2C brands once your pixel has 500+ events; let the creative do the targeting.
